Michael Messana
Pianist & Composer
Music is the revolution. Music is the food our souls crave when nothing else is there to feed it.
Michael L. Messana

Michael Messana composes from the underlying momentum that creates life. His music is homage to the great and unique positions life takes in humanity, and a reminder of its everlasting condition.

In his compositions, he captures the condensation, the rupture, the reverie of pattern; a new self arises from what once was, and every piece is that arrival - revealing and transporting space as an ambient frequency.
